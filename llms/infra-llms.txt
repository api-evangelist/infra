# Infra

> Infra is open-source authentication and access management for infrastructure — it grants short-lived, identity-based access to Kubernetes clusters, servers, and databases through OIDC identity providers. The hosted service (api.infrahq.com) has wound down; the source remains available under Elastic License 2.0 (core) and MIT (SDK + connectors). Final release: v0.21.0.

## APIs

- [Infra API](https://github.com/infrahq/infra/blob/main/docs/reference/api.md): Manage users, groups, grants, destinations, identity providers, and access keys. Base URL https://api.infrahq.com. Auth: `Authorization: Bearer <access key>` + `Infra-Version` header.

## Specs

- [OpenAPI 3.0](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/infrahq/infra/main/docs/api/openapi3.json): 27 paths, 47 operations.
